About the Website "Danppun"

Originally named "Beol's Space". Danppun's Website was created to be a space on the internet where I could share my original characters (Beol being the character I was more fond of at the time) and also be the most creative posible. I changed the name very quickly, I noticed it started being more about me than any of my characters, but you can still see Beol as the icon of the page.

I like to take inspiration on other's designs, so here I say if you want to take a look at my html, go ahead, of course I take hours on making the pages but I don't feel like is that much so I can "claim" them, study my code as much as you want.
